About

The neighbourhood takeaway, plain and proud.

A Taste of China sits at the top of Dunstable High Street North — number 159, a few doors from the corner. The kitchen runs the kind of menu Dunstable expects of a Chinese takeaway: a long one. Duck, lamb, beef, chicken, king prawn, vegetable, and a full sheet of curries, chow meins, chop sueys and sweet & sour.

Cooking is to order. Boxes are filled by the wok. Pick up at the counter or get it brought to the door anywhere in LU1, LU3, LU4, LU5, LU6 or LU7. No marketing, no fuss — just the food, served the way the neighbourhood has come to like it.
